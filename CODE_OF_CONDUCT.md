# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of acceptable behavior include empathy, respectful communication, constructive
feedback, and accepting responsibility for mistakes. Unacceptable behavior includes
harassment, personal attacks, trolling, discriminatory language, and publishing others'
private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior through the repository's private security reporting
channel. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this code.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
